About Shyama Prasad Smriti Sangha

✎ Draft — words not finalised yet

Shyama Prasad Smriti Sangha is a community club founded in memory of the great patriot and visionary leader Dr. Shyama Prasad Mukherjee. We are a group of dedicated individuals committed to preserving his ideals of national unity, cultural pride, and service to the community.

The club organises cultural programmes, community welfare activities, and annual commemorations to honour Dr. Mukherjee's legacy and inspire future generations.

Our Namesake — Dr. Shyama Prasad Mukherjee

Dr. Shyama Prasad Mukherjee
1901 – 1953

Dr. Shyama Prasad Mukherjee

6 July 1901  –  23 June 1953
Barrister Educationalist Politician Hindutva Activist Union Minister

A barrister trained at Lincoln's Inn, London, Dr. Shyama Prasad Mukherjee became Vice-Chancellor of Calcutta University at just 33 — the youngest in its history. His founding of the Bharatiya Jana Sangh laid the ideological groundwork for what would become the Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP).

Such was his stature that Prime Minister Nehru appointed him Minister of Industry and Supply despite their political differences. He later entered Jammu & Kashmir in protest of its special status, was arrested, and passed away in custody in Srinagar on 23 June 1953 — circumstances that remain a subject of national debate to this day.

"I will not allow India to be divided again — even if I have to sacrifice my life."

IN MEMORIAM

Late Club Secretary — Narendra Nath Halder

Narendra Nath Halder
1929 – 2020

Narendra Nath Halder

1 February 1929, Kolkata  –  30 December 2020, Kolkata
Club Secretary Telecom Officer, GOI Social Worker Community Leader

Narendra Nath Halder was the founding Secretary of Shyama Prasad Smriti Sangha and one of the true pillars of this community. Born in Kolkata on 1st February 1929, he served the nation with dedication as an officer in the Telecommunications Department of India under the Government of India — contributing through decades of the country's growth and development.

Beyond his government service, he was widely respected in this locality for his selfless social work — always at the forefront of community welfare, cultural events, and the upliftment of those around him. As Secretary of this club, he was the driving force that kept the spirit of Dr. Shyama Prasad Mukherjee's ideals alive in our neighbourhood.

He left us on 30th December 2020, but his values, his warmth, and his lifelong service to this community remain our eternal guiding light. We dedicate this club to his memory.
