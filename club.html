About Shyama Prasad Smriti Sangha

✎ Draft — words not finalised yet

Shyama Prasad Smriti Sangha is a community club founded in memory of the great patriot and visionary leader Dr. Shyama Prasad Mukherjee. We are a group of dedicated individuals committed to preserving his ideals of national unity, cultural pride, and service to the community.

The club organises cultural programmes, community welfare activities, and annual commemorations to honour Dr. Mukherjee's legacy and inspire future generations.

Dr. Shyama Prasad Mukherjee

6 July 1901  –  23 June 1953
Barrister Educationalist Politician Hindutva Activist Union Minister

Dr. Shyama Prasad Mukherjee was one of the most towering figures of post-independence India — a barrister trained at Lincoln's Inn, London, who returned home to become Vice-Chancellor of Calcutta University at just 33 years of age, the youngest in its history.

A passionate educationalist and a fierce nationalist, he rose to prominence as a powerful voice for the cultural and political rights of Hindus in undivided Bengal. His founding of the Bharatiya Jana Sangh laid the ideological groundwork for what would eventually become the Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP).

Such was the respect he commanded across party lines that Prime Minister Jawaharlal Nehru — despite Mukherjee's principled and unwavering opposition to the Congress — appointed him as the first Minister of Industry and Supply in independent India's Union Cabinet.

His steadfast opposition to the special status granted to Jammu & Kashmir led him to enter the state in protest, where he was arrested. He passed away in custody in Srinagar on 23 June 1953, under circumstances that remain a subject of national debate to this day.

"I will not allow India to be divided again — even if I have to sacrifice my life."
